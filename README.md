## 📌 Project Overview

This project is part of my **IBM Data Analytics coursework**, focused on analyzing a **fleet equipment inventory dataset using Microsoft Excel**.

The project demonstrates how raw operational data can be cleaned, organized, summarized, and transformed into meaningful **business insights using Excel Pivot Tables and data aggregation techniques**.

The analysis focuses on understanding **equipment distribution, departmental resource allocation, equipment types, and opportunities for improving fleet utilization and operational efficiency**.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Clean and organize the raw fleet equipment dataset
- Analyze equipment distribution across departments
- Identify patterns in equipment ownership and allocation
- Compare equipment types across departments
- Identify potential resource allocation gaps
- Generate actionable business recommendations
- Demonstrate practical Excel-based business analysis

## 📊 Key Analysis & Insights

### 1️⃣ Department-wise Equipment Distribution

The **Transportation department** holds the largest share of equipment, with approximately **1,221 units**, significantly higher than several other departments.

Departments such as **Permitting Services** and the **Sheriff's Office** have comparatively fewer assets.

**💡 Business Insight:**  
The high concentration of equipment in Transportation indicates a strong operational dependency on fleet resources and highlights the importance of effective fleet utilization and monitoring.

---

### 2️⃣ Equipment Type Analysis

The dataset shows a strong presence of **heavy-duty and transportation-related equipment**, while categories such as **Compact Utility Vehicles (CUVs)** appear less frequently.

**💡 Business Insight:**  
The equipment mix suggests that the organization relies significantly on high-capacity assets to support operational activities.

---

### 3️⃣ Uneven Resource Allocation

Equipment is not distributed evenly across departments. Some departments maintain substantially more assets than others.

**💡 Business Insight:**  
This difference may be driven by variations in operational demand, but it could also indicate opportunities to review whether certain assets are underutilized.

---

### 4️⃣ Multi-Department Equipment Usage

Certain equipment categories are used by multiple departments.

**💡 Business Insight:**  
Shared equipment can potentially be managed through centralized scheduling or resource-sharing strategies, reducing the need for departments to maintain duplicate assets.

---

### 5️⃣ Operational Efficiency Opportunities

The concentration of equipment within specific departments creates both opportunities and risks.

Potential opportunities include:

- Improved fleet coordination
- Better equipment scheduling
- Identification of underutilized assets
- Shared-resource strategies
- Improved maintenance planning
- Potential reduction in unnecessary fleet costs

---

## 💼 Business Recommendations

### 🔹 Optimize Fleet Distribution

Review equipment allocation across departments and identify opportunities to rebalance resources based on actual operational requirements.

### 🔹 Monitor Equipment Utilization

Track how frequently individual assets are used to identify underutilized or idle equipment.

### 🔹 Identify Cost-Saving Opportunities

Evaluate whether underused assets can be reassigned, shared, consolidated, or retired.

### 🔹 Implement Shared Resource Strategies

Where appropriate, allow departments to share certain equipment instead of maintaining separate assets.

### 🔹 Improve Fleet Monitoring

Develop an interactive **Power BI or Tableau dashboard** to monitor fleet distribution, utilization, maintenance, and departmental requirements.
